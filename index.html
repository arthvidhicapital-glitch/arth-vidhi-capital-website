Our approach

Learn • Analyse • Decide carefully

Use a repeatable process rather than reacting to noise. These are educational steps, not individual investment recommendations.

STEP 01

Learn the basics

Understand the instrument, business, market context, and the risks involved.

STEP 02

Study the evidence

Review reliable public information, assumptions, financial trends, and valuation limits.

STEP 03

Respect uncertainty

Consider loss capacity, diversification, time horizon, and the possibility of being wrong.
